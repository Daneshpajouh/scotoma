The supplementary measurements quantify five enumeration choices; their two-order
spread is measured, not inferred from the covering theorem. A nomination should
declare assembly and version, mismatch threshold, gap and PAM policy, and number
nominated with number assayed \citep{kalter2025offtarget,fda2026ngs}.
Bulge and mismatch scope makes recall an upper bound, this panel lacks
exchangeability, and its assay counts establish no population effect or safety guarantee.
The companion retains the unrestricted fractional boundaries \citep{companion_reach}.

\paragraph{Interpreting a missing nomination.}
Guide and emitter fix the pattern deficit; genome and budget determine lost loci. The annotation
and single-implementation mismatch-matched read-depth comparisons detect no separation on the
tested covariates, and neither rank omitted sites nor establish equivalence; a negative assay on
nominated candidates cannot account for un-nominated ones.
