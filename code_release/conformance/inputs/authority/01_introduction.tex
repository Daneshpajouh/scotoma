\paperheading{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
In enumeration-based validation, a search nominates off-target candidates and a subset
is assayed. The reported number assayed out of number nominated depends on search
class, assembly and treatment of sequence absent from it. Mismatch, bulge and PAM
policies change the list \citep{kalter2025offtarget,park2016casoffinderbulge}.
A bulge is an unpaired base or contiguous stretch in the guide--site alignment.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
We prove an unrestricted covering floor and the unique minimum sound pattern family
theorem, and derive the $2016$ Cas-OFFinder bulge wrapper's
\citep{bae2014casoffinder} positional deficit for every bulge size. Under soundness, every accepted word lies in the complete deletion union; the
unique minimum holds for $m\le n-3$, including wildcard competitors. The elementary
construction gives $K=r$ for $n\ge20$, $m\le5$; the companion's weighting theorem extends
this to $m\le9$ \citep{companion_reach}. Fractional thresholds and integer obstructions
belong to the companion. Tool audit and assembly measurements connect omitted patterns to
nomination losses at clinical guide sg1617 ($4{,}414\to4{,}778$), $129$ CHANGE-seq
\citep{lazzarotto2020changeseq} sites and five Tracking-seq2 cellular loci.

\paragraph{Why the omission matters for nomination.}
Expansion inserts wildcards for unpaired DNA bases ($n$ non-degenerate patterns) or
deletes guide bases for unpaired RNA bases ($r$ words), then matches within a substitution
budget \citep{park2016casoffinderbulge}. Deletions within a maximal run coincide: the
run-count identity gives one word per run. The audited sources fix their sets without
matching lower bounds against freely placed centres
\citep{park2016casoffinderbulge,casoffinder_source}. The executed $2016$ wrapper omits the
terminal RNA placement; restoring it on clinical guide sg1617 raises the count from
$4{,}414$ to $4{,}778$ loci ($364$ corrected-only, all position~$20$ at four mismatches).
At four mismatches the same omission excludes $129$ measured-active CHANGE-seq rows on
$40$ guides before targeted testing. CRISPRseek $1.52.0$'s default dedicated bulge route
(backend $2.4.1$), executed in R, emits $1{,}491/1{,}634$ distinct words and is short on
$98/110$ spacers, missing only endpoint words---the generator's design, not a crash.
Tracking-seq2 \citep{cong2026tracking2} supplies five cellular loci of one guide that the
same terminal omission alone reaches at four under NGG. Section~\ref{ssec:tool-audit}
separates source reading, parser execution and native search.

\paragraph{Activity context and prior diagnoses.}
At matched $\le4$ mismatches, bulges account for $55.7\%$ of active in vitro off-target
rows ($23{,}692/42{,}572$) in the CHANGE-seq tables redistributed with CRISPR-Bulge
\citep{lazzarotto2020changeseq,yaish2024bulges}, versus $25.8\%$ and $27.6\%$ in the
GUIDE-seq deposits ($312$ of $1{,}207$ and $147$ of $533$); those candidates were enumerated by alignment, not by the expansion we
audit \citep{yaish2023swoffinder,yaish2024bulges}. Earlier work reports incomplete bulge
search or proves completeness for a new aligner
\citep{yaish2023swoffinder,yaish2024bulges}. CRISPRitz identifies a different defect
between two bulges \citep{cancellieri2020crispritz}; CALITAS treats mixed-gap restrictions
\citep{fennell2021calitas}. A preprint reports $7{,}860$ off-targets the expansion fails to
return and leaves the cause open \citep{labun2025chopoff}. We do not claim those are ours:
their first printed missed site needs a two-sided bulge, a different limitation, and the
full list is not deposited.

\paragraph{Scope and release.}
These are sequence-similarity nominations, with no cleavage or safety inference.
Genome-wide CHANGE-seq and GUIDE-seq do not begin from an enumerated list
\citep{lazzarotto2020changeseq,tsai2015guideseq}, so zero of $459$ deposited
bulge-bearing cellular actives being affected is the expected control for that design;
the positive cellular case is the Tracking-seq2 five-locus set above, with the NGG/NGA
scope stated in the abstract. We release \toolname, an enumerator for haplotype-resolved
assemblies, with soundness checks and audit artifacts at \repourl. The appendix retains
remaining measurements, algorithms and fixtures.
