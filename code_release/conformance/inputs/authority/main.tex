\author{\authorblock}

  pdfauthor={Amirhossein Daneshpajouh, Megan Fowler, Kay C. Wiese}}
\maketitle
\thispagestyle{empty}

\begin{abstract}
A bulge-aware CRISPR off-target search expands a guide into shorter patterns and matches
each within a substitution budget. The bulge is not a refinement at that budget: of the
$42{,}572$ off-target sites on the CHANGE-seq panel with measured \emph{in vitro} activity
within four mismatches, $23{,}692$ carry one, which is $55.7$ per cent, and in two cellular
GUIDE-seq deposits the share at the same budget is $312$ of $1{,}207$ and $147$ of $533$.
What allowing one
costs is a configuration choice and not a property of the guide: on the $110$-guide panel at
four mismatches under NGG, one single-base bulge multiplies the candidate count by $35$, a
union through two bases by $108$, a mixed DNA and RNA class by $188$ and the union of all
three by $276$, a $7.9$-fold spread at fixed panel, assembly, PAM and mismatch budget.

Under the soundness requirement that every accepted word belong to the radius-$m$ union of
the $r$ distinct single-deletion words of a length-$n$ guide, that deletion library is the
unique minimum sound pattern family for $m\le n-3$, even against wildcard competitors. An
elementary scattered-transversal construction proves the unrestricted floor $K=r$ for
$n\ge20$, $m\le5$; the companion's weighting theorem extends it through $m\le9$
\citep{companion_reach}. The minimum is the run count, read off the guide in linear time
with no search, so a tool's own count of distinct deletion words decides completeness
before any genome is scanned.

The audited $2016$ bulge wrapper is short of $r$ on $98$ of the $110$ panel guides, and we
give its positional deficit in closed form for every bulge size. The terminal deletion
position holds $583$ sites with measured \emph{in vitro} activity; $260$ sit on guides the
omission exposes, and
$129$ of those, on $40$ guides, are reached by no other pattern it emits within the budget
and by no bulge-free alignment within that budget either. Against the $323$ sites it spares at that
position, the $129$ do not separate on gene overlap ($60.5$ against $58.8$ per cent, Fisher
$p=0.83$) or on protein-coding overlap ($48.8$ against $51.4$, $p=0.68$), and at the
four-mismatch boundary no read-depth difference is detected (median $14$ against $16$ over
the $213$ spared rows at that count, $p=0.33$; $p=0.015$ against the whole spared set, a
comparison confounded by mismatch count).

Executed instances scope the consequence. On clinical guide sg1617 the released wrapper
reports $4{,}414$ loci and a corrected build $4{,}778$, the $364$ added all at spacer
position~$20$; over GRCh38 primary the difference is $34{,}941$ loci of one alignment
class; in two deposited indel training sets all $238{,}705$ RNA-bulge rows place the
deletion at positions $2$ to $19$, while the endpoint words reach $35{,}272$ loci no
interior word reaches; CRISPRseek $1.52.0$ emits $1{,}491$ of the $1{,}634$ words this
panel requires; Cas-OFFinder 3.0.0b3 supports bulges natively and returns all twenty DNA
patterns, yet its executed parser omits RNA position~$20$ and so misses the same $129$; and
five Tracking-seq2 loci of one guide, active in $293$T cells, are reached at four
substitutions only by deleting position~$20$ under NGG, two of the five being
ungapped-reachable at four if NGA is admitted, and one of the five amplicon-confirmed by
its authors. We release
the enumerator, its soundness checks and the audit artifacts. All figures are nomination
counts, not population safety rates.
\end{abstract}
