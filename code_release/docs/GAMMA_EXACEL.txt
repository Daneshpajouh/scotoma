READING. At k = 1 the certificate costs 95 per cent of the candidate set at delta1 = 0.05:
a census in all but name, which is the price of a zero-false-alarm claim when a single
missed site would break it. The price falls fast in k. The operative question for a trial is
not 'is this guide clean' but 'if it is not, at how many sites at minimum', which is a
mechanism question about the editing enzyme and the assay, not something the validation
data can answer (Proposition 2 of the paper).
