The published encoder expects `_` for biological gaps and `-` for zero-vector padding. Convert only deposited alignment `-` characters to `_`, with every other character unchanged. Both 23-character inputs are then independently left-padded by one encoder `-` to form a 24-column, seven-channel tensor. Guide `N` copies the DNA character in the same column, including the deposited `NNN` PAM mask. The original encoder must exactly reproduce every converted tensor before any score is computed. An unconverted alignment gap fails the gap-versus-padding negative control.
