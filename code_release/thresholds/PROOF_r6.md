# PROVED: the six-run threshold

Date: 2026-10-29. Alphabet size four; N=n-1.

**PROVED, by an exact computer-assisted certificate and the supplied upper bounds.**

\[
\boxed{M(n-1,6)=\left\lfloor\frac{3n-12}{5}\right\rfloor
                 +\mathbf 1_{\{n=12\}},\qquad n\ge10.}
\]

The premises reused from the question are E4 (restriction), E13 (the infinite
six-run strict-shortness family), E16 (budget monotonicity), and E21 (the exact
five-run threshold). Their proofs are not rerun here. The new part is a
full-mass construction at the six-run boundary for every n>=13.

## 1. PROVED: an eighteen-orbit construction

Set C=n-6 and b=floor((3C+6)/5). Each listed prefix denotes a separate orbit.
X means an independently uniform letter in {2,3}. The tail is uniform over
all four-letter words of length C having weight b-d. Assign **total orbit
mass**, rather than individual-word mass, as follows.

| Prefix pair | d | Mass per prefix |
|---|---:|---:|
| 00000, 11111 | 0 | 19/37 |
| 00011, 00111 | 0 | 16/37 |
| 01000, 11101 | 1 | 15/74 |
| 01110, 10001 | 1 | 19/74 |
| 011X0, 1X001 | 2 | 6/37 |
| 011X1, 0X001 | 1 | 21/74 |
| 011XX, XX001 | 2 | 11/37 |
| 10000, 11110 | 1 | 21/74 |
| X001X, X011X | 2 | 21/37 |

**PROVED by direct prefix distances.** Every prefix has a unique closest
library prefix at distance d. After adding its weight-(b-d) tail, every
supported word is private and has distance exactly b from its assigned
library word. Every private region receives mass one. Hence the total mass
is six. For C>=7 all tail weights lie in [0,C]. The verifier checks these
statements from the prefixes, without trusting their displayed d values.

## 2. PROVED: exact loads and prefix reduction

For a weight-w uniform tail and a fixed weight-p centre tail, let S be the
reduction in distance relative to the zero tail. Then

\[
T_{C,w,p}(k)=\Pr(S\ge k)
=\frac{R(C,w,p,k)}{\binom Cp3^p},
\]

\[
R(C,w,p,k)=\sum_{a=0}^p\binom wa\binom{C-w}{p-a}3^{p-a}
                 \sum_{e=\max(0,p+k-a)}^a\binom ae2^{a-e}.
\]

**PROVED by counting.** Among the p changed centre positions, a are occupied
by the supported tail and e match the centre letters, so S=a+e-p. This yields
the formula, including thresholds outside the possible gain range.

For a centre prefix c in {0,1,X}^5, define H[d,delta] to be 148 times the supported
mass at assigned distance d and prefix distance delta from c, with X assignments
averaged. These are integers. Its exact load is

\[
\ell(c,p)=\frac1{148}\sum_{d=0}^2\sum_{\delta=0}^5
          H_c[d,\delta]\,T_{C,b-d,p}(\delta-d).
\]

**VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY.** The 243 centre prefixes produce 126 distinct
profiles. There are 37 maximal profiles under cumulative domination within
each d block. Every profile is dominated by one of these 37 actual profiles.
Since T decreases with delta, cumulative domination dominates the load by
summation by parts. All profiles and the domination map are regenerated by
`prove_family.py`; none are presumed from stored output.

## 3. PROVED: all tail distances p<=53

Write C=5q+s, s in {0,...,4}. Then b=3q+floor((3s+6)/5). For every maximal profile put

\[
F_{s,p,H}(q)=148\binom{5q+s}{p}3^p-
 \sum_{d,\delta}H[d,\delta]R(5q+s,b-d,p,\delta-d).
\]

**PROVED.** F is a rational polynomial in q of degree at most p: each term
contains a binomial polynomial of degree a times one of degree p-a. On its
integer domain C>=7,C>=p, those falling-factorial polynomials agree with the
binomial counts, including their zeros.

Let q_0=max(0,ceil((7-s)/5),ceil((p-s)/5)). Newton's identity gives

\[
F(q_0+z)=\sum_{k=0}^p\Delta^kF(q_0)\binom zk\quad(z\ge0).
\]

**VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY.** All forward-difference coefficients are
nonnegative for all five residues, all 54 distances p=0,...,53, and all 37
maximal profiles: 9,990 polynomials and 274,725 coefficients. There are no
shifted starting points or skipped exceptions. A further evaluation checks
the degree-p reconstruction for every polynomial. Every arithmetic operation
in the certificate is an integer operation or a `Fraction` operation.

**PROVED.** The nonnegative Newton expansion gives F>=0 at every admissible
integer q, establishing all these loads at every C>=7.

## 4. PROVED: all tail distances p>=54

Take t=7/5. Sampling the p centre positions without replacement gives

\[
\mathbb E[t^S]\le
\left[\frac{1-w/C}{t}+\frac wC\frac{2+t}{3}\right]^p.
\]

**PROVED.** This is the elementary symmetric mean inequality for the population
of w copies of (2+t)/3 and C-w copies of 1/t. Pairwise equalization increases
each elementary symmetric sum at fixed population sum, proving the bound
without an independence assumption.

Acceptance at prefix distance delta from a support point assigned distance d
requires S>=delta-d. Markov's inequality therefore bounds its share by
t^(d-delta) times the displayed moment. For C>=p>=54,

\[
w/C\le b/C\le3/5+6/(5C)\le28/45,
\qquad h=\frac{4607}{4725}<1.
\]

**VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY, exactly.** The maximum prefix multiplier over all
243 prefixes is 1308/343, and

\[
\frac{1308}{343}\left(\frac{4607}{4725}\right)^{54}<\frac{99}{100}<1.
\]

**PROVED.** Thus every remaining load is strictly below one. Together with
the finite polynomial certificate, this is a feasible packing of mass six
for every n>=13 at radius b. The library supplies a cover of mass six, and E16
carries equality to every smaller radius.

## 5. PROVED: the matching upper bound and small cases

Let b_5=floor((3n-11)/5) and b_6=floor((3n-12)/5). Except when n congruent_to 2 mod 5 they agree,
so E4 and E21 give M(n-1,6)<=b_6. In the exceptional residue with n>=17,
write n=17+5z. E13 gives a strictly short cover at m=8+3z=b_6+1.
E16 again gives M<=b_6. These are upper bounds on the full-weight threshold;
no integral-cover conclusion is inferred from a strict upper bound on tau*.

**VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY.** The small thresholds are M(9,6)=3,
M(10,6)=4, and M(11,6)=5, with both boundary certificates in `certificates/`.
The last is the one exception to b_6. These finite checks and the preceding
infinite proof establish the boxed formula.
